Capri includes third-party Python packages and native libraries.
Their distributed metadata and license notices are in THIRD-PARTY/ and
package-specific directories under _internal/. Supplemental license texts
for Qt for Python are in licenses/.

PySide6 Essentials 6.12.0 declares the following license expression in its
package metadata: LGPL-3.0-only OR GPL-2.0-only OR GPL-3.0-only.
PySide6, Qt and Shiboken are distributed as shared libraries under _internal/.
Compatible shared libraries can be replaced there without rebuilding Capri.

Qt for Python source:
https://github.com/pyside/pyside-setup/tree/v6.12.0
Qt open-source downloads and source information:
https://www.qt.io/download-open-source

Models and inference engines are downloaded separately by the application.
Their licenses are supplied by their respective upstream projects.
