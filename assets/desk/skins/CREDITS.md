# Skin art

- `capri/`: her official look (standing, sitting, eyes closed), drawn for Capri by **xilia_world00** on Discord.
- `watcher/`: the Watcher (a secret skin), drawn for Capri by **xilia_world00** on Discord.

The original sprites in `../sprites` are by [Lachy](https://github.com/lachydotmcg) (PolyForm
Noncommercial 1.0.0, see `../LICENSE.md`).

A skin is a folder with the same sprite sheets as `../sprites` (same names, sizes and frame layout). Add it to
`capri/skins.py` to list it in her Skins menu.
