# Skin art

- `capri/`: her official look, drawn for Capri by Hibura / the Capri team.
- `watcher/`: the Watcher (a secret skin), drawn for Capri by Hibura / the Capri team.

A skin is a folder with the same sprite sheets as `capri/` (same names, sizes and frame layout). Add it to
`capri/skins.py` to list it in her Skins menu.
